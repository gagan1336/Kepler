## Full Description

KEPLER is your daily intelligence layer for the Indian stock market.

Turn market data, news, sectors, stocks and IPO activity into focused research.

Know what is happening. Understand why it matters. Investigate deeper.

---

**MARKET INTELLIGENCE**

Understand what is happening across the Indian market.

Track Nifty, Sensex, market movements, global markets, commodities, currency and crypto — all in one place.

---

**AI MARKET NEWS**

Cut through information overload.

KEPLER collects and processes market news to help you understand what happened, why it matters, which sectors may be affected, and which stocks are connected.

---

**STOCK SCREENER**

Explore thousands of Indian stocks using fundamental and market data.

Screen by P/E, P/B, ROE, ROA, margins, momentum, valuation metrics and other available financial indicators.

---

**BREAKOUT INTELLIGENCE**

Discover stocks showing technical breakout characteristics based on available market data.

---

**SECTOR INTELLIGENCE**

Understand how major Indian sectors are moving.

Explore sector performance, sector leaders, sector laggards, sector news and related stocks.

---

**IPO INTELLIGENCE**

Track upcoming and active IPOs.

Explore available GMP, subscription data, issue information, IPO dates and market context.

---

**AI DEEP DIVE**

Go deeper into individual companies.

Explore business snapshot, financial quality, valuation context, momentum, key developments, recent news and risks to watch.

---

**BUILT FOR RESEARCH**

KEPLER is designed to help investors research faster and understand market context in one place.

Know what is happening.
Understand why it matters.
Investigate deeper.

---

**IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER**

KEPLER is an educational and informational market intelligence platform.

KEPLER is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst. Nothing in the application constitutes personalized investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell securities, or a guaranteed outcome.

Always consult a qualified financial adviser before making investment decisions.
