## 💡 Journaling Habits

- Log every trade **immediately after closing it** — not at end of day
- Be honest about mistakes — the journal is your coach, not your judge
- Review weekly every **Sunday** — spot patterns in wins and losses
- Review monthly — look for your best and worst performing setups
