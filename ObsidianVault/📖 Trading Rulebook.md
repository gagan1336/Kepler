# 📖 Trading Rulebook

> These are my personal trading rules. Reading this before the market opens keeps me disciplined.

---

## ⚖️ Risk Management Rules

1. **Max risk per trade**: 2% of total capital
2. **Max open positions**: 5 at any time
3. **Max daily loss limit**: 3% of capital — stop trading for the day if hit
4. **Always set a stop loss before entering** — no exceptions
5. **Never average a losing trade**
6. **Never move stop loss further away** — only trail it in profit

---

## 📐 Entry Rules

1. Only enter trades with a **minimum 1:2 Risk:Reward**
2. Enter only when price is near a **key level** (support/resistance/breakout)
3. Wait for **price confirmation** — no anticipating
4. Check volume — prefer trades with **above-average volume**
5. Check the broader market trend before entering

---

## 🚪 Exit Rules

1. **Take partial profits** at Target 1 — move SL to breakeven
2. Let the remaining position ride to Target 2
3. Exit immediately if stop loss is hit — **no hoping**
4. If trade is flat after 2 sessions, consider exiting (opportunity cost)

---

## 🧠 Psychology Rules

1. No trading when emotional (angry, anxious, overexcited)
2. No revenge trading after a loss
3. No FOMO trading — if I missed a move, let it go
4. No overtrading — quality setups only
5. Stick to the plan — don't make decisions mid-trade without a clear reason

---

## 📅 Routine Rules

1. **Pre-market** (9:00–9:15 AM): Review global markets, check news, mark key levels
2. **During market**: Focus only on pre-defined watchlist stocks
3. **Post-market**: Log all trades, update portfolio, journal observations
4. **Weekly review** (Sunday): Review all trades, identify patterns, plan next week
5. **Monthly review**: Analyze P&L, adjust strategy if needed

---

## 🚫 What I Don't Do

- No tips or recommendations from social media
- No penny stocks or illiquid stocks
- No trading without a proper setup
- No doubling down on losing positions
- No trading in the first 15 minutes (9:15–9:30 AM) without clear reason
